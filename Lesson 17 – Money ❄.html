Lesson 17 – Money

1 · Grammar

Second conditional – unreal present/future: If + past simple, would + verb.
If I inherited a fortune, I would invest it.
Wish + past simple – regret about now: I wish I had a bigger emergency fund. (were for all persons; wish … would = annoyance/change)
Present perfect – have/has + V3 – past with present result or life experience: She has squandered her savings.
